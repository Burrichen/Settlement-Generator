Interactive settlement maps, the no-code generator framework, Notable Location subtypes and revamped names.

> **Before you update:** this version upgrades your saved data so it can store maps. A verified backup is made automatically first, and settings, saved settlements, favourites and your table and tag edits are all kept. Once upgraded, v0.1.5 can no longer open the data; to go back, restore the backup from the `backups` folder beside your data.

**Maps**

- New **Map** tab on saved settlements. Trading Posts, Villages and Towns get one map; Cities and Capitals get one map per district, each drawn when you ask for it.
- Zoom, pan, and click any building to see what it is in the **Location Inspector**: its type, condition, who lives there and a short description. Details are rolled the first time you open a building and kept after that. You can edit, regenerate or reset them, and your own changes are protected.
- Put the settlement's **Notable Locations** on buildings, or add your own. Each gets a numbered pin, and the numbers never change. Add your own **points of interest** too.
- A **Legend** and a settlement-wide **Directory** jump straight to any location, in any district. Districts can be renamed, and each district remembers where you left it.
- **Export** maps as PNG pictures, SVG drawings or map data, one district or all of them. **Export** and **Import** whole settlements, with all their maps, as a single file.

**Generator framework**

- **Generation is now a flow** you can see and change: the Settlement Pipeline is a list of steps (roll a table, conditions, repeat, run another flow).
- **Table Editor:** create and edit tables without JSON: results, dice and chances, rerolls, follow-up rolls, modifiers, Shift and Cap, and layouts that match the chances you want.
- **Flow Editor**, and editors for **Tags**, **Settings** and **Modifiers**. Bare Minimum Buildings is now an ordinary setting, and every modifier is explained in plain English.
- **Test any table or flow:** roll once, step through, or run it 100, 1,000 or 10,000 times and compare how often each result came up with the chance the dice should give.
- **Roll details (advanced)** in Settings shows how every value was reached.
- **Import / Export** tables, tags, settings, flows or a whole content pack as a file. Imports are checked before anything changes.
- **Safe deleting:** every item shows what uses it, and nothing that is still in use can be deleted.

**Notable Locations and names**

- Hospitality, Trade / Shop and Craft / Workshop locations now roll a **subtype** (such as Tavern / Inn or Jeweller / Goldsmith), with Magic Store specialities and Weaponsmith / Armoursmith splits. Subtypes and their specialities show on the maps.
- Bare Minimum Buildings adds a **Tavern / Inn** with 4 spare rooms.
- Larger, revamped **name packs** for the regions (Bratislor, Halingo, Hjartheland, Nareth, Ordon, Shinkira, Targoth) and species, with gendered given names and family names. Skgurr settlement names now use d100 Geographic Compound tables.

The terminal version has all of the above, with Maps and Building screens that work without drawing the map itself.

Known limitations:

- The building details tables (types, conditions, descriptions) are placeholders and will be replaced.
- Maps are schematic: ring streets and rectangular buildings, with no shared layout between a city's districts.
- Shared results, scales and object types have no editor yet.
- A City saved before districts existed cannot be mapped until its Notable Locations are rolled again.
