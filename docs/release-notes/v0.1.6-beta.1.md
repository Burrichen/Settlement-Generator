**Beta: interactive settlement maps.** A test build for trying out maps. It is not offered as an automatic update, and is not meant for everyday use yet.

> **Before you install:** this beta upgrades your saved data so it can store maps. A backup is made automatically first, but **once a beta has opened your data, v0.1.5 can no longer open it**. To go back, restore the backup (in the `backups` folder beside your data) or reinstall this beta. If your settlements matter to you, try the beta on a copy of your data, or wait for the stable release.

- New **Map** tab on saved settlements:
  - Trading Posts, Villages and Towns get one map;
  - Cities and Capitals get one map per district, each drawn when you ask for it.
- Zoom, pan, and click any building to see what it is in the **Location Inspector**: its type, condition, who lives there and a short description. Details are rolled the first time you open a building and kept after that. You can edit, regenerate or reset them, and your own changes are protected.
- Put the settlement's **Notable Locations** on buildings, or add your own (for example a castle). Each gets a numbered pin, and the numbers never change. Add your own **points of interest** too.
- A **Legend** and a settlement-wide **Directory** jump straight to any location, in any district. Districts can be renamed, and each district remembers where you left it.
- **Export** maps as PNG pictures, SVG drawings or map data, one district or all of them. **Export** and **Import** whole settlements, with all their maps, as a single file.
- The terminal version has **Maps** and **Building** screens for all of the above, without drawing the map itself.
- Location subtypes (such as Tavern / Inn or Jeweller / Goldsmith) and their specialities show on the maps.
- Settlements saved with earlier versions open unchanged and can be given maps at any time. A City saved before districts existed cannot be mapped until its Notable Locations are rolled again.

Known limitations in this beta:

- The building details tables (types, conditions, descriptions) are placeholders and will be replaced.
- Maps are schematic: ring streets and rectangular buildings, with no shared layout between a city's districts.
- Exports go to the **Settlement Generator** folder in your Documents; the folder cannot be chosen yet.
- This installer is not signed for automatic updates. When the stable version is released, this beta will be offered it as a normal update.

See [the maps guide](https://github.com/Burrichen/Settlement-Generator/blob/feature/interactive-settlement-maps/docs/MAPS_GUIDE.md) for how to use maps.
