Settlement naming.

- New **Name** stage, straight after Region, in Automatic, Incremental and One-table generation and when editing a saved settlement.
- All eight Kurovian regions have their own naming scheme, each with a Geographic Compound and a Historical Founder method, rolled with real dice:
  - Ordon (Alderwick, Godwinsbury);
  - Halingo (Khar Well, Varesh's Well);
  - Skgurr (Dunwick, Fergus's Point);
  - Shinkira (Kirisawa, Takedamura);
  - Bratislor (Drevgrad, Vorelgrad);
  - Hjartheland (Nordvik, Eirikheim);
  - Nareth (Naroneth, Talarien);
  - Targoth (Karhold, Varok's Gate).
- Roll a name, choose a naming method, reroll just the name, or type your own. A typed name is always possible.
- The save dialog offers the generated name. Saved settlements keep the full name record and are never renamed by later changes.
- Every naming table can be customised in Settings → View Tables. Tag View shows how a name was chosen and every roll.
- Other Setting Profiles have no naming data yet: there you name settlements yourself, as before.

The regional naming lists other than Ordon are provisional sample data, not official Kurovian lore.
